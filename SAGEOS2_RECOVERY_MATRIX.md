## Architectural overreach findings

1. The coordinator itself is not the regression. Its explicit turn/generation/state guards are clearer and candidate 206 closes a real recursive loop. The overreach is connecting it to an incomplete speech backend and partial action vocabulary, then treating structural completion as product parity.
2. The home rewrite improved conversational emphasis, but the rewrite also severed working capability surfaces. Hiding debug UI did not require deleting normal-owner functionality.
3. The structured twin model improves durable identity data, but moving the owner's authoritative Core into generic notes weakened ordering semantics unnecessarily.
4. Lazy Brain loading fixed startup crashes and should remain. Candidate 203's 4,096 context/output inflation was not needed for identity and was correctly reverted.
5. Root/Forge tool contracts are valid infrastructure boundaries. They do not replace ordinary Android actions, Owner App navigation, workflows, or local read-only tools.
6. Isolated KWS is a valid stability change. Hard-coding only two wake phrases and silently dropping legacy command behavior is not required by isolation.
7. Advanced/Diagnostics separation improves the home. Product features such as memories, Owner Apps, wake profiles, voice choice, and workflows must remain owner-accessible without becoming debug panels.
